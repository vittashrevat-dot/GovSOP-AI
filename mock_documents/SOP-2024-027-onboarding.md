## Purpose
This procedure standardizes the steps for bringing new employees into the
agency so they are productive and compliant from day one.

## Before the Start Date
Human Resources confirms the offer and start date. Information Technology
provisions accounts, a laptop, and building access ahead of arrival.

## First Week
New hires complete orientation, mandatory security awareness training, and an
introduction to their team. Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy.

## First Month
The manager sets initial goals and schedules a 30-day check-in to review
progress and address any gaps.
