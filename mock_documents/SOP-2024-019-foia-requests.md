## Purpose
This procedure describes how the agency receives, tracks, and responds to
requests for public information.

## Intake
Requests may arrive by mail, email, or the public portal. Every request is
logged in the intake tracker on the day it is received and assigned an owner.

## Response Timeline
The agency acknowledges each request within three business days and provides a
substantive response within 20 business days. Complex requests may be extended
with written notice to the requester.

## Redaction
Exempt information is redacted in line with the applicable statute before
release. The Legal department reviews all redactions.
