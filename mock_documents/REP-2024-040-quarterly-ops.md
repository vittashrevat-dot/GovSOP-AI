## Summary
This report covers operational performance for the second quarter, including
service availability, facilities status, and continuity readiness.

## Service Availability
Core services met the 99.5 percent availability target. One planned outage was
executed during the maintenance window with no unplanned downtime.

## Facilities
Site B requires a backup generator to serve as the secondary continuity
location. Procurement is scheduled for the third quarter.

## Continuity
A continuity drill validated failover to Site B. Minor gaps in contact lists
were identified and corrected.
