## Purpose
This policy governs the creation, maintenance, and preservation of official
records regardless of format.

## Classification
Records are classified by subject and assigned a retention schedule at the
point of creation. The schedule references the 2018 retention matrix, which has
since been superseded.

## Digitization
Physical records must be digitized within 90 days of creation and the paper
original transferred to the central registry.

## Access
Access to restricted records requires written authorization from the records
custodian. This clause predates the current data classification circular and
needs reconciliation.
