## Attendees
The Budget Committee convened with representatives from Finance, Operations,
and Human Resources. The Finance Director chaired the meeting.

## Budget Status
The committee reviewed year-to-date spending against the approved budget.
Operations reported an overrun driven by facilities maintenance.

## Decisions
Discretionary spending across all departments is frozen until the Q3 review is
complete. A revised forecast will be circulated the following week.

## Action Items
Each department will submit cost savings proposals before the next session.
