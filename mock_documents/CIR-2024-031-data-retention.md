## Purpose
This circular communicates updated requirements for retaining and disposing of
official electronic records.

## Retention Tiers
Records are classified into three tiers. Tier 1 covers legal and financial
records retained for 10 years. Tier 2 covers operational records retained for
5 years. Tier 3 covers transient records retained for 1 year.

## Disposal
Expired records must be disposed of through the approved secure deletion
workflow. Disposal events are logged and reviewed quarterly.

## Backups
Tier 1 data must be stored in encrypted backups with access restricted to the
Information Technology custodian group.
