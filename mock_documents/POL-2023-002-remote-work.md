## Purpose
This policy establishes the conditions under which employees may perform their
duties from a location other than their assigned office.

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probationary period and whose roles do not
require daily on-site presence may request a remote work arrangement.

## Core Hours
Remote employees must be available during core collaboration hours of
10:00 to 15:00 local time for meetings and coordination.

## Equipment and Security
The agency provides a laptop and VPN access. Employees must not store official
documents on personal devices. This clause references the retired 2019 security
standard and is pending update.
