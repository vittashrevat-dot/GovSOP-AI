## Purpose
These guidelines ensure that digital content published by the agency is
accessible to people with disabilities.

## Standards
All public-facing digital content must conform to WCAG 2.1 level AA. This
covers perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust content criteria.

## Images and Media
Every informative image requires descriptive alternative text. Video content
must include captions and, where feasible, an audio description track.

## Color and Contrast
Text must maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5 to 1 against its background.
Color must not be the only means of conveying information.
