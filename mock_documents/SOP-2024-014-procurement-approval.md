## Purpose
This procedure defines how procurement requests are reviewed and approved
across the agency. It applies to all departments initiating the purchase of
goods or services funded by the operating budget.

## Scope
The procedure covers purchases from initial requisition through final payment.
Capital projects above $250,000 follow the separate Capital Expenditure Policy.

## Approval Thresholds
Purchases up to $10,000 may be approved by a department manager. Purchases
above $10,000 require Finance review. Purchases above $25,000 require two
authorized signatures, one of which must be the Finance Director.

## Vendor Registration
Vendors must be registered in the procurement system before any purchase order
is issued. Registration renewals are due by the end of each quarter.
