## Purpose
This procedure describes how the agency detects, reports, and responds to
information security incidents.

## Severity Levels
Incidents are rated from Sev-1 (critical, agency-wide impact) to Sev-4
(minor, isolated impact). The severity determines the response timeline and
escalation path.

## Reporting
Any suspected incident must be reported to the security team within one hour of
discovery. The on-call responder acknowledges and begins triage.

## Notification
Confirmed data breaches must be reported to affected parties and the Legal
department within 48 hours of confirmation.
