# Product

**GovSOP AI** is a local-first enterprise AI document management and decision
support prototype for government agencies.

## Problem

Agencies manage thousands of documents (policies, SOPs, circulars, guidelines,
reports, meeting minutes). Finding the right information is slow, which hurts
decision-making and productivity.

## Who it's for

Government employees and stakeholders who need to find authoritative information
quickly and act on it.

## Key features

1. **AI Search & Decision Engine (RAG-lite)** — natural-language search over the
   document library with explicit source citations (document ID + section).
2. **Auto-Organizing Directory & Compliance Alerts** — a filterable document
   directory showing department and effective date, with status-driven badges
   (⚠️ Needs Review, Outdated Clause). Supports drag-and-drop upload.
3. **Intelligent Extraction Dashboard** — action items, deadlines, responsible
   departments, and key policy changes extracted into structured cards, each
   linking back to its source document.

## Principles

- **Local-first and offline.** No API keys or external services; search and
  extraction run entirely on the local machine.
- **Traceable.** Every answer and extracted item points back to a source
  document and section.
